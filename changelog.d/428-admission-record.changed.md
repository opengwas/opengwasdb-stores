- **Every build step passes `--admission-record <artifact-root>/<id>/store.opengwasdb.admission` (issue #428, opengwasdb#357, opengwasdb ADR 0072).** From opengwasdb 0.5.0, every builder refuses to run without it, and writes there what each Analysis examined, admitted and dropped. `plan()` adds it to the `build-dense-vcf`, `build-hybrid`, `build-ragged-ssf` and `build-ragged-besd` steps. It names the Store's final path, never the staged `.partial`, and refuses a recipe option that sets the same flag. The record is kept beside the release's step records and is not published. A completion writes none. A forced run's snapshot and archive do not cover the record. Every `build_command` in `stores.tsv` gains the pair.
