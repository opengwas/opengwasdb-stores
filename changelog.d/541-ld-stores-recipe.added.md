- **The HGDP+1kGP LD Stores and Genotype Stores are built from this repository, and the build writes their records (#541, epic #496).** `pixi run ld-stores build --kind ld_store|genotype_store --panel <P> --out <dir> --ld-id OGL-NNNNN` stages a Genotype Store's inputs as ogld#92's `build92.sh` did (read-only copies, each checked against the published LD Store's provenance: `source.sha256`, and `adjustment.pc_file.sha256` for the PC File), runs `opengwasdb ld build` with the Genotype Stores' flags (Partner Rows at `t_partner` 0.01) and `opengwasdb ld validate` in build92.sh's environment, and writes the record with its own `rebuild` through the writer's new `--rebuild` option (validated, and never changed silently). It refuses a checkout with uncommitted changes or untracked files, so `rebuild.version` names a commit. `pixi run ld-stores rebuild OGL-NNNNN --out <dir>` rebuilds a recorded Store into a new directory from that section, validated once, then accepts it on a field-by-field comparison of `opengwasdb ld info`, failing on any difference outside a closed list of the build's own fields and five PC Check scan statistics held to 1e-9 relative, and on what an HGDP+1kGP Store's provenance must record. OGL-00007 to OGL-00011 are rebuildable; the other recorded Stores, built by `scripts/build_hgdp1kgp.py`, `ogld derive-partners` or `ogld import`, are refused as not runnable, and OGL-00001 to OGL-00005 cannot be rebuilt equal: they have no Partner Rows, which the engine now requires.
