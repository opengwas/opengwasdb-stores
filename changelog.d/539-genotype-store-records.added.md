The LD Store Registry records Genotype Stores (#539, epic #496). The writer infers a record's `kind` from the provenance (`filesets` makes a `genotype_store`, and `filesets` without `inputs` or `arrays` is refused), records `measured.genotype_bytes` from a stat of the pgen, psam and pvar files the provenance pins (a pin that does not resolve or whose size differs is refused), and `ld-resources.tsv` gives such a Store `pgen + sidecar, r computed per query`. OGL-00007 to OGL-00011 are the five HGDP+1kGP Genotype Stores.
