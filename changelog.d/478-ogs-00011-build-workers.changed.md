- **OGS-00011's build runs with 10 workers, not 24 (#478).** The forced rebuild exhausted the 1,006 GB host 4 h in: the 24 `build-hybrid` workers held 811 GB together (about 34 GB each), and the host's LD API held another 411 GB. On the owner's decision it was stopped, and the published Store and its records were left untouched. The change is execution-only (opengwasdb ADR 0053: the worker count changes no stored value), recorded in `docs/ogs-00011-nworkers-10-amendment.md`. The build-phase `validate` follows the build's count. Completion keeps 24, because it caps its own pools by available memory (opengwasdb ADR 0080).
