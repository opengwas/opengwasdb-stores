- **The LD-panel tools and the Store audit follow opengwasdb#381's reference-first indel ALIDs (ADR 0079 §6).** A panel sorts an indel's alleles and declares no reference, so its id names no ALID: `verify_block` now checks that an indel's id parses, as its sorted spelling is the `EA < OA` rule, and round-trips only SNVs and substitutions; `compare_eur_imputation` leaves a panel indel out of the comparison instead of matching its sorted spelling (a deletion's is the insertion's) to a Store's reference-first ALIDs. `audit` no longer reports a 0.2.2 deletion (`1:827587:GGGGCC:G`) as an ALID not in canonical allele order. Without this a block with an indel failed `verify_block`'s `alids` check under the pinned engine.
