- **A completion's register no longer requires build-phase records of post steps added after the build published (#532).** `register_release` required a record for every earlier-phase step of the current plan, so a recipe amended with `post.clumps` or `post.rho` after the Observed-Only build published made `completion-register` refuse with `MissingRecordError` after the whole completion had run (OGS-00024). An earlier-phase step with no record that the earlier register's `inputs` do not list is now skipped; any other missing earlier record still refuses. `build_elapsed_s` stays the sum over the steps that ran.
