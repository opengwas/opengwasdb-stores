Epic: publish, rebuild and Reference-Complete the full Store Releases under their own ids

Purpose

Finish the full Store Releases: - publish the Stores that are built; - rebuild the Stores that are stale or still at format 0.1.0; - Reference-Complete every Store except MVP META under its own id (ADR 0037).

Published, rebuilt and Reference-Completed the full Store Releases under their own ids: the MVP observed Stores OGS-00012..16, OGS-00006/09/11 brought to validated, FinnGen OGS-00017 rebuilt, OGS-00011 rebuilt with its 3,169 Analyses, and OGS-00011, OGS-00012, OGS-00013 (AFR), OGS-00014 (AMR), OGS-00015 (EAS) and OGS-00017 completed under single ids. The run found that every HGDP+1kGP LD panel held the other allele’s frequency and duplicated variant identities (#348); the five panels were repaired in place, OGS-00001/02 rebuilt and re-completed, OGS-00014’s reference EAF flipped and its top-hit SE re-synced (#409), and every completion since was checked for orientation against an independent reference. Every Store it published is in the regenerated release benchmark, measured on a quiet host; OGS-00006’s completion (#224) waits on opengwasdb#301.
