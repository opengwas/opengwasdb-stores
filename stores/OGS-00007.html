Release notes

First 10 Sun et al. INTERVAL SomaScan plasma-proteome pQTL analyses (PMID 29875488) – a deterministic prefix of the 100-analysis 2018-sun-pilot-100 selection, built end-to-end through OpenGWASDB’s real ragged builder as a small, current, grounded proof that this Store Family’s pipeline (Trait Ontology Mapping, CURIE-normalised trait_ontology_id, analysis_label) actually produces a working store.

Deliberately not a replacement for 2018-sun-pilot-100 – same source, same selection basis, just a small independent slice used to prove the build end-to-end without re-running the full 100-analysis pipeline.

Status is `built`, not `validated`: the store built successfully and reads back correctly, but validation.yaml correctly reports passed_with_warnings – opengwasdb’s Ragged builder (opengwasdb.layouts.ragged.build_ssf) silently drops analysis_label/trait_ontology_id/trait_ontology_label into the built store despite the manifest supplying real values (filed as explodecomputer/opengwasdb#83). This is a real upstream gap, not a defect in this release; left failing rather than silently accepted.

Release Erratum (issue #130): the registry manifest was migrated from the retired trait_id/gene_id/gene_name Analysis columns to analysis_label plus an Ensembl trait_ontology_id/trait_ontology_label, using the same tracked analysis_targets.tsv evidence. No Store artifact was rebuilt or rewritten by this registry migration; the existing validation warnings remain the truthful record of the earlier build. Release Erratum (issue #133): assigned_ancestry was recorded as the free-text Source Ancestry Label “European” rather than the registry-normalised super-population code “EUR”. Corrected in analyses.tsv. The quantitative Analyses also stored n_cases/n_controls as 0 where the counts are not applicable; those cells are now empty (absence is distinct from zero). source_ancestry_label remains “European” as source provenance. No Store artifact was rebuilt or rewritten. Release Erratum (issue #141): trait_ontology_id held “ENSEMBL:<gene>” and trait_ontology_label held “Ensembl” on all 10 Analysis rows, replacing the source-provided EFO terms – a gene identifier where a Trait Ontology term belongs, and an authority name where a trait label belongs (introduced by issue #130). The source-supplied EFO term and its trait label from the tracked SomaScan target sidecar are restored, with trait_ontology_mapping_method=source_provided. GCST005806’s restored term EFO:0021984 is the genuine source term and is obsolete; it is recorded as source_provided rather than replaced with an invented non-obsolete term. GCST90240123 is an aggregate assay over the seven-member 14-3-3 family: its restored term is the source’s EFO:0020109 “14-3-3 protein family measurement”, and its analysis_label is now the SomaScan SeqId 4707-50 rather than the single member gene YWHAH that SomaScan.db maps the probe to (somascan_is_multiple is 0 for this SeqId, so the source label’s own seven-member list is the tracked evidence that identifies the aggregate). The gene/Ensembl identity remains target annotation in trait_chr/trait_bp and the sidecar. No Store artifact was rebuilt or rewritten.
