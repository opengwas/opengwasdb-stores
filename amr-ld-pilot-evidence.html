AMR LD calibration pilot: evidence (#241)

Status: calibration evidence for the owner’s AMR decision in #229. This is not a production panel, and the run claims no PASS. No production panel, population mapping, block definition or Store was changed.

Source and cache

All five chromosome VCFs declare GRCh38 and answer an indexed region query. All 1,116 AMR and EUR-control members appear in every header. The receipt records each VCF’s size, mtime, cache marker and tabix-index SHA-256.

The cached per-ancestry PGENs cannot serve the pilot: - they exist only for AFR, EAS, EUR and SAS; - they were converted with MAC >= 50, a 7.2% MAF filter at n = 345.

The raw VCFs are unfiltered, so the MAF 0.01 floor is supported and there is no blocker. Each block region is read by index for the 345 members and converted with no frequency floor. The floor is then applied once, in construct_block.

It checks: - one row per eigenvector row, with unique canonical ALIDs; - finite, non-negative, descending eigenvalues; - agreement with provenance, and EAF; - exact row and allele binding (||X v_j||^2 = λ_j, relative error ≤ 2e-15); - loading through core’s list_all_blocks and load_ld_eigenvectors.

Held-out genotype calibration

The pilot may read reference genotypes only, so its GWAS is simulated (tools/ld-panel/heldout_calibration.py): 1. Split the 345 into 230 training and 115 held-out individuals, stratified by population. 2. Build the panel from the training individuals. 3. Draw each replicate’s z-scores with the held-out individuals’ own LD: one to three causal variants scaled to a peak |z| of 8, plus N(0, R_test) noise. 4. Mask 20% of variants and impute them with core’s impute_z_block (truncation 0.9, min_cor 0.7).

A panel built from the held-out individuals themselves gives the in-sample upper bound. Each block ran 20 replicates per panel.

Here, the “GWAS” LD is itself a 115-person estimate, which makes the metric pessimistic. It is also optimistic: training and held-out individuals come from the same four populations, which need not match MVP’s Hispanic/Latin American ancestry mix.

Findings outside the AMR question

  1. The shipped HGDP+1kGP panels hold two rows under one ALID.
    • Cause: gnomAD splits multi-allelic sites, so at a homopolymer an insertion G>GA and a deletion GA>G at one position both canonicalise to pos:G:GA.
    • Extent: every block of AFR, EAS, SAS and the EUR control has pairs. That is 291,411, 116,794, 141,543 and 134,043 duplicated ALIDs, 1.3–2.0% of rows.
    • Effect: core maps both rows to one Store variant. #228 (AFR) and #230 (EAS) complete against these panels.
    • The pilot excluded every record of a shared ALID: 9–16% of above-floor records in these blocks, mostly homopolymer indels. After the owner’s decision below, construct_block keeps the more frequent record instead. The shipped panels are unchanged; #348 tracks them.
    • UKB EUR (ld/EUR) has no duplicates on chr22.
  2. A MAF floor over called individuals admits sparsely called sites (see Retained variants above). The MAC ≥ 50 floor at n ≈ 800 kept most of them out implicitly.

Limitations

  • n = 345 caps every block at rank 344. Calibration trains on 230.
  • Calibration is simulated from reference genotypes. No GWAS association data was read, and #241 requires a separate read plan before any is.
  • One related pair (kinship 0.069) is retained. No relatedness pruning sensitivity was run.
  • Neither FILTER nor missingness was filtered. ALID collisions were excluded.
  • This covers five blocks. Nothing here estimates genome-wide quality or cost.

Measured recommendation for the next stage

This is evidence for the owner’s decision, not acceptance.

  1. Integrity: the tooling builds AMR blocks at MAF 0.01 that verify exactly, load in core and are resolved at the consumer’s 0.9 truncation. Mechanically, a genome-wide build is ready.
  2. Decide variant QC before any genome-wide build. The pilot shows three choices that change the panel:
    • a missingness ceiling: 8–14% of retained variants are more than 50% missing outside the MHC;
    • FILTER PASS-only: 4–16% of retained variants are non-PASS;
    • the ALID-collision rule: currently every record is excluded.
  3. The accuracy evidence cannot yet support a production call. Held-out median r is 0.80–0.88 outside the MHC, but the metric has no anchor. Two ways to close that:
    • Run the same held-out design on a 345-person subsample of the EUR control cohort at these blocks. That anchors the metric to an ancestry whose real-GWAS performance is known (0.963 at n = 771). It reads reference genotypes only.
    • Run a masked-variant evaluation on OGS-00014’s AMR-assigned Analyses. This needs an association read plan.
  4. Boundaries: EUR boundaries serve a calibration build. Because AMR shows more weak crossing LD at most edges, a production gate should add the AFR-boundary sensitivity and an imputation test across an edge.
  5. Shipped panels: the duplicated-ALID defect in the shipped panels should be resolved before #228 and #230 complete against them.
